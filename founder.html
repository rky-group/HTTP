About the Founder

Ramkumar Krishnasamy (RKY)

RKY Group was founded by Ramkumar Krishnasamy, whose vision is shaped by an enduring interest in leadership, nation-building and the power of long-term thinking.

Inspired by the example and wisdom of Lee Kuan Yew (LKY), Ramkumar hopes to carry forward lessons in vision, discipline, institution-building and forward planning into the future through his own entrepreneurial journey.

“Build with purpose, learn from experience, and think beyond the present.”
A long-term perspective

Inspired by leadership and the future

The founder’s ambition for RKY Group is to build a platform for business development, continuous learning and the creation of opportunities. The journey is guided by the belief that meaningful progress takes patience, resilience and a clear sense of purpose.

This outlook continues to inform the group’s evolving portfolio and its interest in connecting people, expertise and ideas.
