About the Founder

Building with Purpose. Thinking Beyond the Present.

Inspired by long-term thinking, institution building and service to society.

Ramkumar Krishnasamy (RKY) founded RKY Group with the belief that businesses can be more than commercial enterprises — they can become instruments for expanding access to technology, opportunity, financial participation and a better quality of life.

The founder's thinking has been deeply influenced by the ideas and legacy of Lee Kuan Yew (LKY) — particularly the importance of long-term thinking, institution building, discipline, pragmatism and the belief that meaningful progress requires sustained effort across generations.

RKY does not seek to replicate Singapore or reproduce another country's journey. Instead, the intention is to carry forward enduring ideas: think beyond the immediate, build strong foundations, invest in people, create capable institutions and pursue progress that can benefit society over the long term.

This philosophy forms part of the foundation behind RKY Group — building businesses today while keeping an eye on the opportunities and responsibilities of tomorrow.

“The future is built not by what we inherit, but by what we choose to build, preserve and pass forward.”
