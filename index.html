GMX
GENERAL MERCHANT INC. UNDER RKY GROUP

A Modern General Merchant for the Digital Economy

Connecting Businesses. Creating Opportunities. Enabling Growth.

Inspired by the historic general merchant concept, GMX is envisioned as a modern business partner that brings together expertise, services, suppliers, digital capabilities and commercial opportunities under one flexible business model.

GMX will initially operate as a consulting-first technology business serving small and medium-sized businesses worldwide. It will use a lean operating model, specialist contractors and trusted partners, with the potential to expand into digital products, managed services, sourcing and broader commercial connections over time.

Explore a Business Partnership →

The General Merchant Principle

Historically, a general merchant connected customers with a broad range of goods, suppliers and services. GMX adapts that coordinating role to the modern business environment: one trusted relationship helping clients find expertise, solve problems and access relevant opportunities.

The Modern GMX Approach

Start with consulting and technology delivery. Build recurring managed services. Develop reusable digital products where customer demand is proven. Expand into sourcing and commercial partnerships selectively, without carrying unnecessary inventory or building a costly fixed organisation at the outset.

About the Founder

Ramkumar Krishnasamy

Founder of RKY Group

Ramkumar Krishnasamy (RKY) founded RKY Group with a vision of building a diversified business group that connects technology, entrepreneurial thinking and commercial opportunities.

With professional experience across technology delivery, project management, business analysis and financial-services technology, he brings a practical, delivery-oriented perspective to business development and digital transformation.

His vision is to build a group of focused businesses that can work independently while benefiting from shared expertise, trusted partnerships and a common commitment to creating long-term value.

Through General Merchant Inc. (GMX), that vision extends to a modern general merchant approach: connecting businesses with services, specialist expertise, technology and commercial opportunities.
