A New Commercial Vision

General Merchant Inc. (GMX)

A modern general merchant for the digital economy.

Inspired by the Spirit of the Historic General Merchant

Throughout commercial history, general merchants helped connect producers, suppliers, buyers and markets. Their value extended beyond selling goods: they built relationships, sourced what customers needed, coordinated transactions and helped commerce move forward.

GMX aims to bring this principle into the modern business environment. Our vision is to build a broad commercial house that helps businesses access the expertise, technology, products, services and partnerships they need to operate and grow.

Beginning with IT consulting, business services and supplier sourcing, GMX intends to expand into managed services, commercial trade, distribution and digital business solutions as opportunities develop.

One Commercial House. Multiple Opportunities.

GMX will connect customers with the right expertise, source suitable solutions, coordinate trusted partners and develop lasting business relationships — starting lean and expanding in response to real customer demand.

01

Consulting & Advisory

IT consulting, project and programme management, business analysis, digital transformation, technology implementation support and business process improvement.

02

Trade & Sourcing

Connecting buyers with trusted suppliers, identifying products and service providers, coordinating quotations and facilitating commercial partnerships.

03

Managed Services

Coordinating ongoing IT support, cloud administration, reporting, software services and specialist business operations through qualified delivery partners.

04

Commerce & Distribution

Exploring business supplies, technology products, wholesale opportunities, distribution partnerships and supplier-led fulfilment based on validated demand.

05

Digital Products & Platforms

Developing opportunities for business software, subscription services, digital tools and online platforms that connect businesses with relevant products and service providers.

06

Global Business Connections

Building relationships between businesses, suppliers, consultants and commercial partners to support sourcing, collaboration and international market opportunities.

Our Business Model

GMX will adopt a lean, partnership-led approach. We will initially generate revenue through consulting, sourcing and project-based services, then build recurring income through managed services and expand into selected commercial trading opportunities.

Over time, proven services and customer relationships may support digital products, online marketplaces and additional business categories. Expansion will be guided by customer demand, partner capability, sustainable margins and disciplined investment.

About the Founder

Ramkumar Krishnasamy (RKY) founded RKY Group with a vision to build businesses that connect people, ideas, technology and commercial opportunities.

Influenced by the leadership, strategic thinking and nation-building philosophy of Lee Kuan Yew, RKY aspires to carry forward the values of long-term vision, institution-building, discipline and practical execution into the future.

This philosophy is reflected in the ambition behind RKY Group: to develop enduring businesses, create meaningful commercial relationships and pursue growth through innovation, responsibility and collaboration.

Through its evolving portfolio of ventures, RKY Group seeks to turn ideas into practical businesses and build a platform for future generations of enterprise.
